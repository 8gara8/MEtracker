# Day 274 brief — US disables a blockade runner as Trump touts an extra carrier, Iran claims a mined tanker and retaken ships, and Kuwait holds Tehran responsible

*Saturday, November 28, 2026 · Day 274 of the US-Israel war on Iran*

## Executive Summary

Washington tightened the blockade as Tehran raised its claims at sea. CENTCOM says a US aircraft disabled the Palau-flagged bulk carrier Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday after it left Bandar Abbas and ignored repeated warnings, crew unharmed; it is the sixth ship disabled since July and the first since Iranian officials vowed to break the blockade. Speaking to troops on Thanksgiving, Donald Trump said the US had sent "an extra aircraft carrier," that victory "will happen whenever I want it to happen," and that Pickaxe Mountain would be a target if strikes resume; he does not think Iran will make a deal. Iran answered with claims that cannot be checked. The IRGC Navy says a tanker on an "unauthorized route" north of Ghanam Island struck a mine early Friday and a second turned back; navy commander Shahram Irani says commandos retook two Iranian tankers seized by US forces, naming none. Supreme Leader adviser Mohammad Mokhber says Hormuz stays shut. Kuwait summoned Iran's ambassador and holds Tehran "fully responsible" for the drone attack that killed two Italians; Qatar's deputy emir also blamed Iran, while Abbas Araghchi called the incident "a precooked recipe for war." In Islamabad, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey pledged to intensify support for the kingdom against Houthi attacks. In Israel, Gadi Eisenkot vowed to form a government within eight days. Iraq closed more crossings with Iran under US pressure. Brent fell 2.5% to about $94; hormuz.now counts 1.7 tanker crossings a day against 47.6 pre-crisis. No new casualties were adopted. Direction stays escalating; the probability holds at 5.

## Strategic Implications

**1. The blockade hardens as Iran's sea claims outrun the evidence.**

Analytical judgment: under the multi-clock framework, the energy-infrastructure and active-deadline clocks moved most. CENTCOM's disabling of the Pacific Archer after it left Bandar Abbas is the first enforcement strike since Mokhber, Araghchi and Mohsen Rezaei publicly committed Iran to breaking the blockade; CENTCOM counts six ships disabled and 158 diverted since July. Iran replied with claims, not confirmed hulls. The IRGC Navy says a tanker on an unauthorized route north of Ghanam Island hit a mine early Friday; Irani says commandos retook two Iranian tankers seized by US forces, and Tasnim carries his claim that a missile hit the USS Abraham Lincoln on the war's first day. None names a vessel or is corroborated; UKMTO's only fresh report is an unspecified incident between a cargo ship and military forces in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday, consistent with the Pacific Archer strike. The mines-as-enforcement and claim-inflation priors hold, and the blockade-as-trigger prior tightens: Tehran has made breaking the blockade declared policy while striking truckers, now in about 20 cities, protest fuel shortages the government itself blames on it. Irani's claim that the US got not one ship through Hormuz and JMIC's count of 128 US-facilitated passages between 22 and 25 November, via Shafaq, cannot both be true; hormuz.now's AIS count of 1.7 tanker crossings a day fits neither.

**2. Trump's carrier and Eisenkot's eight days frame the decision window.**

Analytical judgment: the active-deadline and political-will clocks now run together. Trump told troops the US had sent an extra carrier, said victory would come "whenever I want it to happen," named Pickaxe Mountain as a target if action resumes and said he did not think Iran would deal. These are statements of intent, not a decision: no carrier was named and no Pentagon announcement was located. They sit beside Thursday's i24NEWS report that he leans toward waiting for an Israeli government, and that marker now has a date. Eisenkot says he will form a government within the eight days left on his mandate, which expires 6 December; his bloc has 36 signed seats, 48 with Bennett, and needs UTJ or Ra'am for 61, while Netanyahu is reportedly ready to offer him the first turn in a rotation. The coalition-clock prior strengthens; the caretaker-risk prior holds until a swearing-in. On negotiation capacity, Araghchi said, via Tasnim, that Iran is "fully prepared" to defend itself and open to diplomacy if its demands are met, and called the Kuwait deaths "a precooked recipe for war"; Kuwait summoned Iran's ambassador and holds Tehran fully responsible. The deniable-host-strikes prior holds. The Mecca alliance's Islamabad meeting pledged to intensify Saudi Arabia's defence and, per Fidan, discussed US-Iran talks; Russia's Georgy Borisenko says Iraq, Iran and the Gulf states are open to Moscow's Gulf-security proposal. Mediators multiply, but none has moved the terms. The probability holds at 5.

For Taipower and CPC the exposure remains winter LNG, not crude. Trump's extra carrier and a decision window tied to Israeli coalition timing fall across January and February cargo planning.
