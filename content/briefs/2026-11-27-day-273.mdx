# Day 273 brief — Iranian drone debris kills two Italians in Kuwait as Tehran denies the attack, the IRGC claims more mined tankers and a Houthi strike kills a worker at Jazan

*Friday, November 27, 2026 · Day 273 of the US-Israel war on Iran*

## Executive Summary

Iran's attacks on its Gulf neighbours turned deadly for Europeans. Kuwait's Defence Ministry says debris from Iranian drones intercepted on Thursday killed two Italian technicians, from Rheinmetall Italia, servicing an air-defence radar at a "vital site," and wounded a Kuwaiti officer and a Pakistani national. Italy's foreign minister Antonio Tajani said Rome would lodge a complaint with Iran's embassy; European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called for more pressure on Tehran; Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, the UAE and Jordan condemned the strike. Iran denies involvement: armed forces spokesman Abolfazl Shekarchi said no operation was conducted, and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi offered Kuwait a joint investigation. At sea, the IRGC Navy claims two more tankers, one a supertanker, hit mines north of Khasab early Thursday; no hull is named, while UKMTO separately reports an outbound tanker struck by a projectile 18 nautical miles east of Khasab on Wednesday, crew safe. IRGC Navy chief Ali Ozmaei calls Iran's control of Hormuz "an operational reality." The Saudi-Houthi war drew blood: a projectile on the Jazan refinery killed a Nepalese worker, and the coalition says it destroyed 50 Houthi targets. On the decision clock, i24NEWS reports Trump leaning toward waiting until Israel forms a government, as Gadi Eisenkot agreed a coalition deal with the Democrats and says Iran poses no immediate threat. A Washington institute reports Iran rapidly building an underground complex near Isfahan. Brent rose a third session to about $96; hormuz.now still counts 2.4 tanker crossings a day against 47.6 pre-crisis. Other-theatre deaths rise by three. Direction stays escalating; the probability holds at 5.

## Strategic Implications

**1. Kuwait's dead Italians widen the war's audience and test Iran's denial.**

Analytical judgment: under the multi-clock framework, the coalition-cohesion and negotiation-capacity clocks moved most. Kuwait's Defence Ministry says debris from intercepted Iranian drones killed two Rheinmetall Italia technicians servicing a Skyguard air-defence radar, the first European deaths from the Gulf attacks recorded in this series this month. Iran's denial is unusually categorical: armed forces spokesman Abolfazl Shekarchi said no operation was conducted, Araghchi offered Kuwait and Italy a joint investigation, Iran's embassy in Rome raised a "false flag," and Fars, citing an unnamed source, pointed to Ukraine without evidence. Earlier this month Iran openly claimed strikes on US sites in Kuwait after the Qeshm raid, so the denial is a claim to be weighed against that record, not a fact. The reaction is broad: Tajani will lodge a complaint, von der Leyen called for more pressure on Tehran, Kuwait invoked Article 51 and Security Council Resolution 2817, and Qatar, a mediator, warned that the attacks undermine diplomacy. The retaliation-map-widening and strike-the-hosts priors strengthen, and the arguing-to-third-parties prior now cuts against Iran in Europe. Araghchi told IRIB that Qatari and Pakistani mediation continues but that Iran's demands must be met before Hormuz reopens; South Korea's Cho Hyun told him Seoul has no plans to deploy troops. Mediation persists without movement, and a mediator's public rebuke narrows Iran's room. The probability holds at 5.

**2. The decision clock meets Israeli coalition politics while sea claims stay unconfirmed.**

Analytical judgment: the active-deadline clock is now partly an Israeli political clock. i24NEWS, via Iran International, reports Trump leaning toward delaying any strike until Israel forms a government; former deputy national security adviser Victoria Coates said action would come after the new year. That is a report, not a decision, but it fits Trump's "funded to end-January" framing. Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar has signed up Yair Golan's Democrats and Yisrael Beytenu, aims to swear in a government as early as next week, and says he sees "no direct and immediate threat" requiring action first, while Netanyahu presses Washington and IDF spokesman Effie Defrin told Kan the military is ready to strike alone. The security-establishment-restraint and caretaker-risk priors both hold: the weeks before a transfer of power are when unilateral risk peaks. The Institute for Science and International Security reports Iran rapidly building a hardened underground complex at Behjatabad, function unclear, "a nuclear purpose cannot be ruled out"; AEOI chief Mohammad Eslami calls Iran's progress "unstoppable." That feeds the reconstitution prior and the public case for action. At sea, the IRGC's two new mine claims north of Khasab remain uncorroborated, while UKMTO's projectile strike 18 nautical miles east of Khasab is the only independent hull incident; the mines-as-enforcement and claim-inflation priors both apply. The second-chokepoint prior sharpened: a Nepalese worker was killed at the Jazan refinery and the coalition says it struck 50 Houthi targets.

**3. Taiwan: crude climbs to $96 while LNG flows stay near zero.**

Analytical judgment: no fresh Taiwan-specific development today; prior assessments hold. Trading Economics quotes Brent at $96.23, up 1.4% for a third straight gain, with its statistics panel at $95.90, citing Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure and Iran's mine claims, though oil remains down nearly 20% this month on recovering Gulf shipments, an IEA fourth-quarter surplus and a 1.1-million-barrel US crude build. hormuz.now counts 17 AIS-visible tanker crossings in seven days, 2.4 a day against 47.6 pre-crisis, two LNG carriers in 30 days and Ras Laffan calls at 29% of normal; Kpler, via CBS, shows weekly ship transits falling from 217 to 147 between 1 and 23 November, while Trump says flows exceed prewar levels. The oil-priced-as-surplus versus gas-shut prior holds: crude moves around Iran while QatarEnergy's force majeure runs to end-December. For Taipower and CPC the exposure remains winter LNG, not crude; a strike window now linked to Israeli coalition timing and the new year overlaps February cargo planning. South Korea's statement that it has no plans to deploy troops keeps East Asian navies out of the strait for now. Markers: crossings against 2.4 a day, OPEC+ on 29 November, Ras Laffan calls and the unpassed supplementary budget.
