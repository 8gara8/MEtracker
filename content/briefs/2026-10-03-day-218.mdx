# Day 218 brief — The G7 answers the diesel bottleneck, the carrier arrival moves inside the window, and the Flydubai trail leads to an Omani flight ban

*Saturday, October 3, 2026 · Day 218 of the US-Israel war on Iran*

## Executive Summary

⭐ The instrument arrived. G7 members agreed through the International Energy Agency to release 100 million barrels of reserves over four months, with a frontloaded substantial diesel release in the first 20 days — the first new energy instrument since the SPR was exhausted on Day 215, and an answer to the downstream bottleneck this series has carried as unanswered since Day 216 (Al Jazeera, Bloomberg). Diesel averaged $6.37 a gallon on Friday against the $6.52 record of 22 September, with US distillate stocks at a record-low 107.9 million barrels; Trump had threatened to ban American diesel exports if Europe refused, and the EU rejected the threat before agreeing to release. Force moved the other way — forward. Tehran rejected Washington's account that its delegation was expelled, calling the departure scheduled (Saudi Gazette). Britain charged two Iranian nationals over an alleged Yom Kippur plot in Manchester. Iran's parliament finished a bill legislating control of the strait. Direction turns mixed; seven-day risk extreme; spillover critical; the thirty-day probability rises 8 → 11.

## Strategic Implications

**1. The bottleneck got an instrument, and it came from the allies Washington said were not contributing.**

Day 215 recorded the oil-reserve clock running out: the 172-million-barrel American contribution complete, the reserve at its lowest since 1982, a 252.4-million-barrel floor, and Energy Secretary Wright calling another drawdown unlikely. Day 216 added the downstream half — a $6.50 diesel record, record-low Midwest gasoline inventories, the lowest-ever seasonal distillate level — and recorded that no instrument had been announced for it. Day 218 announces one. Per §3.5.5, quantified: 100 million barrels over four months is roughly 830,000 barrels a day, against the 400 million the IEA's 32 members released in March and a US distillate stock at a record-low 107.9 million barrels. The provenance is the analytical content. Wright's charge that European states had contributed "only a fraction" has been carried unanswered since Day 217; the answer is Canada, France, Germany, Italy and the United Kingdom releasing jointly, and doing it after rejecting the president's threat to ban American diesel exports. Analytical judgment: under the multi-clock framework the energy-infrastructure clock turns in for the first time in weeks, and the coalition-cohesion prior inverts on the economic track while holding on the diplomatic one. The reserve-exhaustion prior is answered, not retired — a four-month trickle does not refill a market Capital Economics called structurally tight.

**2. The reinforcement moved inside the window; the attribution moved out of it.**

Day 217 read the carrier flow as a post-election instrument: the Roosevelt and the 13th Marine Expeditionary Unit, roughly 10,000 personnel, arriving end-November, against a competing account placing three strike groups in late October. The competing account was the accurate one. Today's figures name the Makin Island amphibious group rather than the 13th MEU, 7,000 sailors and 2,000 Marines to about 20,000 in theatre by the end of October — before 3 November, not after — plus two Patriot batteries to Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
