# Day 259 brief — Trump says the US does not need a deal and moves Vance off Iran; Tehran answers with deterrence as tankers burn and the IDF tells Eisenkot there is no urgency

*Friday, November 13, 2026 · Day 259 of the US-Israel war on Iran*

## Executive Summary

Washington's reply came as a social media post, not a written answer. President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that "we don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed," claimed "we've got their money" and "we've got their Strait," and told Vice President JD Vance to give his "complete and total attention" to corruption in Democratic-run states instead of Iran talks, per CNBC, NBC and the Times of Israel. A day earlier the Wall Street Journal had reported that Vance was weighing direct talks with security chief Mohsen Rezaei. Trump told Salem News he would use force with "no hesitation" and would count the regime's survival past his term "a defeat." Tehran answered in kind. Rezaei wrote that there was "no safe haven" for any base used against Iran. Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told Al Jazeera, per Tasnim, that distrust of Washington is the main obstacle and that Iran may end "no war, no peace" through deterrence rather than talks. The Assembly of Experts urged a review of Iran's NPT membership and defence doctrine. In Israel, Channels 12 and 13 reported that the army told Gadi Eisenkot, at a briefing with caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, that there is no immediate need to strike Iran. At sea, the IRGC said two supertankers carrying over 4 million barrels were ablaze after a "massive explosion" on the southern route. UKMTO, via gCaptain, reported the Dominica Prosperity hit in the engine room off Ras Al Khaimah with all 22 crew evacuated, and JMIC counted 181 incidents since March. IRNA reported an "enemy" strike on Sirik. Brent jumped about 3% to near $96. Direction stays mixed; the probability falls to 10.

## Strategic Implications

**1. Trump closes the Vance channel; Tehran says deterrence can replace talks.**

Analytical judgment: under the multi-clock framework, the negotiation-capacity clock took its sharpest hit since the midterms. Washington's move was a Truth Social post saying "those days have long since passed," which also redirected Vance, who negotiated the June memorandum and reportedly sought a session with Rezaei. CNBC and NBC confirm the text, but neither found a White House elaboration or a Vance response. The Times of Israel notes the Journal's report that Trump had told aides he was open to Vance reopening talks, so the post may be positioning rather than a formal end of the track. The official-claim rule cuts both ways. Trump's "we've got their Strait" sits beside hormuz.now's 2.9 tanker crossings a day, which shows a strait closed to most traffic, not one under US control. Rezaei's call to "SURRENDER to the seven conditions" and Ghalibaf's claim that deterrence may end the standoff without talks are equally declaratory. Ghalibaf names Qatar and Pakistan, with Russia and China helping, and Qatar's Amir and UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham discussed support for negotiations toward "a comprehensive agreement." Witkoff and Kushner met Putin on Ukraine, and neither side said Iran came up, so the Moscow-as-venue prior stays unconfirmed. The probability falls three points to 10. Qatar's reported window to about 24 November now has no named US negotiator, and NBC reports advisers telling Trump that the window for major combat closes in January.

**2. Tankers burn on the southern route while Israel's army says there is no urgency.**

Analytical judgment: the energy-infrastructure clock worsened again, but the strike clock did not jump. The IRGC said two supertankers on the "unauthorized" southern route are "engulfed in flames" and warned that ships which switch off transponders will "face a similar fate." It named no hull and no cause. UKMTO, via gCaptain, named the Liberia-flagged Dominica Prosperity, hit 14 nautical miles northwest of Ras Al Khaimah with 22 crew evacuated. JMIC records four tanker attacks since its last note but attributes none, so the hull-named-by-the-third-party prior holds and the host-state count reaches 65 days, flagged. The Didamar case shows why: Fars claimed that navigation aids were hit, and a senior Omani official told the Maritime Executive that they were not. IRNA reported an "enemy" strike on Sirik without a target or a toll; no US or Israeli comment was located. The caretaker-risk prior eased slightly. Channels 12 and 13 report that the IDF told Eisenkot there is no immediate urgency, though Israel should join if Trump resumes attacks. Kan says CENTCOM alerted IDF officers to prepare, which CENTCOM reportedly called "mere speculation." The declared-scope prior widened: the Assembly of Experts called a review of NPT membership and defence doctrine an "unavoidable necessity," though the Foreign Ministry restated its NPT commitment this week. Spillover stays proxy-led. The Houthis claim strikes on Aramco in Riyadh, Khamis Mushait and Sharurah; Riyadh is silent on them and is pressing the EU to list the group as terrorist.

**3. Taiwan: no fresh Taiwan-specific development; Brent jumps to about $96 as deal talk ends.**

Analytical judgment: no fresh Taiwan-specific development was located, and prior assessments are unchanged. This cycle confirmed the diplomacy-priced-not-supply prior. Trading Economics shows Brent near $95.8, up about 3.2% on Thursday, and attributes the rally to Trump's post, while volume barely moved. hormuz.now counted 20 tanker crossings in seven days to 12 November: 2.9 a day against 47.6 before the crisis, up from 2.4. No LNG or LPG carrier crossed in seven days, and one of each in 30. JMIC counts about 211 vessels stationary east of the strait, and the IMO's Arsenio Dominguez urged owners to "refrain from commercial activities in the Strait," with 25 seafarers killed. For Taipower's gas-heavy fleet the binding variable remains Qatari LNG, and what changed today is the timing. With the US president saying a deal is unnecessary, late November, when the reported Qatari window and QatarEnergy's force majeure converge, looks less like a reopening date and more like a review date. A rise of about $3 in a day on diplomacy alone means oil-linked fuel costs stay exposed to headlines as well as cargoes. Trump's "we've got their Strait" and Ghalibaf's insistence that Iran and Oman settle the strait's future describe incompatible regimes; under the toll-institutionalization prior, either leaves LNG transits conditional. The Taipower and CPC supplementary budget remains unpassed as far as this file knows.
