# Day 257 brief — Qatar claims progress and a two-week window as Rubio says the blockade works; Tehran denies talks while the IRGC claims two burning tankers and unveils a missile city

*Wednesday, November 11, 2026 · Day 257 of the US-Israel war on Iran*

## Executive Summary

Qatar says the mediation is gaining ground, and Washington says it can wait. Foreign ministry spokesman Ibrahim bin Sultan Al Hashmi said on Tuesday that Qatar's effort had gained momentum, with "progress in the negotiations," and that details would come once the agreement's shape is settled, per The Peninsula. Iran International, citing diplomatic sources, said the Qatari prime minister told G7 ministers in Paris that Doha had secured "at least two weeks" for diplomacy before any US attack. Secretary of State Marco Rubio told reporters that sanctions and the blockade work "so well" that major combat is not needed, while calling Iran's conditions "absurd." Tehran held its line. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called reports of a Rezaei-Vance meeting groundless and said the two-day proposal "remains on the table"; Mohsen Rezaei told Washington to "STOP manufacturing FAKE NEWS about imaginary talks." The IRGC Navy, via Tasnim, said one supertanker hit a mine and a second was stopped by fire in the strait; no ship was named and no casualties were reported. The IRGC unveiled a new underground missile city, and Bloomberg, via Iran International, reported Iran is producing about 50 ballistic missiles a month. Pete Hegseth said he would order every strike again, and the House voted 211-204 to send Thomas Massie's impeachment articles to committee, per NBC News. A Reuters/Ipsos poll found 71% of Americans want the new Congress to end the war. A ballistic missile aimed at Riyadh was intercepted, and UNIFIL said a patrol near Tyre came under fire. Brent held near $92; hormuz.now counted 2.6 crossings a day against 47.6 before the war. Direction stays mixed; the probability rises to 13.

## Strategic Implications

**1. Qatar claims progress and a two-week window; both principals say the terms have not moved.**

Analytical judgment: under the multi-clock framework, the negotiation-capacity clock improved for a second day, on the mediator's word rather than the parties'. Al Hashmi's "progress in the negotiations" is the first claim of substantive movement Doha has made this cycle, and the reported "at least two weeks" would set the first dated window since the lame-duck clock opened: roughly to 24 November, well short of 3 January. Both are graded as claims. The window rests on unnamed diplomats via Iran International, and no US official has confirmed it. The skeptical counter came from both principals. Rubio called Iran's conditions "absurd" and, per four G7 diplomats cited by Reuters, promised "devastating" economic costs while saying Washington has options beyond war or acceptance. Araghchi called a Rezaei-Vance session groundless, and Rezaei's "STOP manufacturing FAKE NEWS" restates the sequencing wall: blockade lifted and assets unfrozen first in Tehran, nuclear terms first in Washington. The newer signal is domestic. A state television debate on Monday, as reported by Iran International, aired the case for talks through intermediaries, and one analyst described a Pakistani idea of 3.67% enrichment in exchange for diluting about 400 kg of 60% uranium. That is an analyst's account, not a position. The probability rises one point to 13 on the progress claim and the window; it falls back if the window lapses without a named session.

**2. Coercion moves to mines, missile cities and Washington's own politics.**

Analytical judgment: Rubio's line that the blockade works "so well" that major combat is not needed reframes the active-deadline clock. US pressure is economic for now, so the escalation trigger shifts from Trump's will to Iran's conduct at sea. That conduct continued: the IRGC Navy says a supertanker struck a mine and a second tanker was stopped by its "wall of fire," both with transponders off. No hull is named and no third party has confirmed either, so the hull-named-by-the-third-party prior holds. MP Esmail Kowsari linked the mining to the Qeshm strikes but said it does not signal a major war. On the missile side, Mousavi's underground missile city, the lifted 2,000 km range limit and Bloomberg's Western intelligence estimate of about 50 missiles a month and at least 1,700 in stock cut against US claims of severe degradation. Hegseth also said 18 Americans died in Epic Fury, against 15 on the Pentagon's series; the gap is held apart. The political-will clock ran both ways: the House killed impeachment 211-204, but Reuters/Ipsos found 71%, including 51% of Republicans, want Congress to end the war. The Israel's-independence prior gained evidence as TASS and Ynet reported a sharp Netanyahu-Trump call on resuming strikes, which the Prime Minister's Office called fake news. Spillover stayed proxy-led: a missile intercepted over Riyadh, a UNIFIL patrol fired on near Tyre, and a forward GCC command approved for Kuwait.

**3. Taiwan: no fresh Taiwan-specific development; crossings fall to 2.6 a day as Brent holds near $92.**

Analytical judgment: no fresh Taiwan-specific development was located, and prior assessments are unchanged. The diplomacy-priced-not-supply prior widened again. Trading Economics shows Brent at 91.76 on Tuesday, down 0.55% after Monday's near-6% fall, and its narrative credits expected diplomacy and claims tanker traffic has picked up. The strait counts say otherwise: hormuz.now recorded 18 tanker crossings in seven days to 10 November, 2.6 a day against 47.6 before the crisis and down from 2.9, with no LNG or LPG carrier; IMF PortWatch put tanker tonnage at 0.1% of normal to 8 November. For Taipower's gas-heavy fleet the binding variable remains Qatari LNG: zero transits and QatarEnergy's force majeure, last reported into early December. The IRGC's mine claims, if true, reinforce the mine-clearance prior: even a signed deal would need weeks of clearance before LNG carriers return, and insurers will price that. A fire on a South Pars platform, which Iran says is under control, sits on the reservoir Qatar shares as the North Field, though no effect on Qatari output is reported. A two-week window, if real, closes before the 9 December Fed meeting and the reported end of QatarEnergy's force majeure, which makes late November the date to watch for winter cargoes. The Taipower and CPC supplementary budget remains unpassed as far as this file knows.
