## Current war status

Day 163 firmed the Hormuz deal's mechanics while widening its price. Iran said the framework of its Oman shipping-route agreement was "finalized" and awaited only Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei's sign-off, and Oman called the talks "positive and constructive" (ABC News, CNN). Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan answered by signing the "Mecca Joint Defence Agreement," an attack-on-one mutual-defense pact including nuclear-armed Pakistan (Washington Post). Brent firmed ~1.5% to ~$83.40; Hormuz stayed effectively closed under naval escort (Trading Economics).
