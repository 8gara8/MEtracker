## Diplomatic state

Day 152 gave the de-escalatory track its first hard-data confirmation on both fronts — a fourth-day pause now acknowledged by Iran's own military, Bab el-Mandeb transits recovering to 28, Brent back at baseline — and, for the first time, an actual Hormuz architecture: Oman's Gulf-backed two-corridor plan with voluntary fees, which regional reporting says the parties are "approaching." Per §3.5.3 and §3.5.6 this is process over agreement and prior over surprise: Washington frames the pause as space for "very deep talks," Tehran flatly denies a negotiation with Washington exists and pairs the diplomacy with new shipping threats, and the "voluntary fee"/northern-corridor design would settle the strait on Iran's terms (the toll-institutionalization prior, verbatim). The architecture is the most developed of the war and still reaches neither Bab el-Mandeb, where the Houthis answer to Yemen's own ledger, nor Israel, outside the US-Iran framework.
