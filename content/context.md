## Current war status

Day 158 opened on the talks Iran says are not happening. Having called off the weekend strikes, President Trump said US-Iran negotiations would begin today (Aug 4) to finalize a deal reopening the Strait of Hormuz and ending Iran's nuclear threat, warned Tehran it had a "last chance" to sign, and added he was "under no time constraint" (CBS News, CNN, NPR). But Israel went to its highest state of readiness, the IDF assessing Iran would fire ballistic missiles even if Israel stayed out of a US strike (Haaretz, Jerusalem Post), and a UKMTO incident off Khasab (crew/vessel safe) kept the maritime front kinetic. Direction holds mixed; 7-day risk critical; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds at 11. Day 158 = forty-eighth day of the 60-day window.
