## Diplomatic state

Day 151 let the de-escalatory track keep developing on both fronts at once for the first time — a strike pause into its third day, an Omani channel calling the region and reporting "progress" on Hormuz, and a Saudi move to reopen Bab el-Mandeb — while every strand stayed a claim or a reroute rather than a signed fact. Per §3.5.3 and §3.5.6 this is process over agreement and prior over surprise: Washington frames the pause as space for talks, Tehran flatly denies a negotiation exists and refuses to call it a ceasefire, Rubio calls Iran "not serious," and the Saudi "resumption" is an announcement against tanker-tracking that shows its crude rerouted through Suez. The architecture is more developed than any prior moment, but neither the Oman corridor nor any London track reaches Bab el-Mandeb, where the Houthis answer to Yemen's own ledger — and the exporter that "reopened" the route is still sailing around it.
