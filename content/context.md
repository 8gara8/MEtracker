## Current war status

Day 176 was the day the "shut strait" was revealed as a US-run convoy. CENTCOM disclosed it has escorted more than 660 million barrels of crude — about 10 mb/d, roughly half the pre-war flow — through Hormuz since early May, aiding ~1,300 vessels in nightly southern-channel convoys under US warships and fighters, made possible by a two-week campaign that degraded Iran's coastal radar and maritime surveillance (CNBC, Axios, Jerusalem Post, Stars and Stripes). The disclosure reframes rather than reopens the strait: normal commercial transit stays dead — Iran killed the bulk carrier Minoan Dignity and seized the tanker Amara enforcing its permit regime, and the IMO logged ~17 Jul–Aug attacks that killed four sailors — but the flow moves under fire at war-risk rates, and Tehran has neither stopped nor endorsed it. The market gave the restored flow no discount: Brent topped $93 and traded near $94 Friday, a second straight weekly gain of >5% (OilPrice, Trading Economics, CNBC). Washington pressed its economic war: Bessent set a Monday (Aug 25) news conference to detail the "economic isolation" plan and urged China to "get with the program," Treasury sanctioned a Hezbollah cash-courier network (Lebanon/Turkey/UAE/Iran), and the UAE suspended all trade and financial transactions with Iran after fresh missile fire (Gulf News, The National, Just Security, CNN, Washington Post). Beijing again refused, ordering firms to ignore the sanctions; Araghchi — fresh from Beijing talks with Wang Yi — called the plan "bound to fail," Pezeshkian pressed to end the war "from a position of power," and Tehran said Iran-Oman Hormuz talks are advancing "positively" (Fox News, Euronews, Al Jazeera). On Gaza the Kushner-Netanyahu demilitarization plan advanced to two working groups but Netanyahu called the roadmap "problematic" pre-Oct 27 election; the IRGC warned any restart would bring "completely different" weapons. Direction holds mixed; 7-day risk critical; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds 5.
