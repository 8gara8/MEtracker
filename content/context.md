## Current war status

Day 202 gave the Saudi-Houthi exchange a confessional dimension. The Saudi-led coalition said air defences destroyed a Houthi drone **south of Mecca** before it entered prohibited airspace over Islam's holiest city — the first such accusation in almost a decade — and spokesman **Turki al-Maliki** called the security of the holy sites **a red line** the kingdom would take deterrent measures to enforce. The Houthis denied it outright: military spokesman **Yahya Saree** said the night's targets were Saudi oil facilities and military bases "far removed from the holy places," and spokesperson Hazem al-Assad called the claim "a worn-out lie." The **OIC's 58 member states** condemned the attacks on Makkah and the Madinah region and backed Saudi deterrent measures — while offering, per CNN, **no sign of military support**. Saree claimed two operations, against **Aramco at Yanbu** and **Khamis Mushait Air Base**, plus a **Saudi F-15 downed over Marib** (video released); Riyadh confirmed none of it. Separately and harder: **Aramco confirmed crude loadings at Yanbu are suspended** and is cancelling/delaying European cargoes — closing the outlet of the Petroline bypass severed inland since Sep 10, so the kingdom's Hormuz alternative is now cut at both ends. Brent nonetheless settled **$105.83 (−2.7%)** from the $109.21 war high, and WTI $102.43 (−3.2%), after US Energy Secretary **Chris Wright** told CNBC the outage was "brief and temporary… measured in days" — against independent satellite-imagery analysts still saying weeks; the Yanbu suspension lifted ICE Brent back toward $108 intraday. The **US House passed a war powers resolution 220-204**, the third of the war and the largest GOP defection yet (Massie, Fitzpatrick, Davidson, Barrett, Miller-Meeks, Nunn, Mace). UN displacement across Yemen rose to **93,864**. Direction escalating; 7-day risk extreme; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds at 2.
