## Current war status

Day 178 is the day the economic war becomes real — the US "economic isolation" plan lands at Treasury Secretary Bessent's 2 p.m. ET Monday (Aug 24) news conference, and both capitals spent the eve pulling in opposite directions. Iran's newly installed hardline SNSC secretary, Mohsen Rezaei, escalated the threat surface on state television: if Gulf neighbors join Washington's economic war, "not a drop of oil will leave the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz," and Iran would "target other oil export routes" — explicitly extending the chokepoint threat beyond Hormuz to the bypass pipelines (Saudi East-West to Yanbu, UAE Fujairah) the Gulf states built to route around the strait; "we will consider them enemies" (CNN, Middle East Eye, Tehran Times). Yet Tehran's president pulled the other way: Pezeshkian defended the lapsed 60-day US memorandum as the "best path" out of a "neither war nor peace" stalemate, insisting "not a single provision amounts to capitulation" and that the SNSC reached consensus on it, while Egyptian FM Badr Abdelatty phoned Araghchi to try to revive the framework (US News/AP, The New Arab, National Herald). Bessent casts the plan as the largest coordinated economic isolation campaign in history, presses allies to "choose sides," says it will "collapse" Iran's economy, and reaffirms there will "likely not be a large-scale kinetic restart"; Trump warned third countries offering Iran a "lifeline" would face consequences (Bloomberg, Al Jazeera, CNBC). Beijing again refused, its foreign ministry (Lin Jian) calling the measures "illegal unilateral sanctions" with "no basis in international law" and ordering Chinese firms to ignore them — China still buys >80% of Iran's exported oil (China-Global South Project, NBC News). The tape held: Brent near $94, US pump prices ~$1/gal up YoY, the CENTCOM escort corridor moving ~10 mb/d (660M+ barrels since May) with commercial Hormuz transit effectively shut. Direction holds mixed; 7-day risk critical; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds 5.
