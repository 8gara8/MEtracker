## Current war status

Day 180 is the day the economic war's deferred blow got a deadline. A day after "Operation Economic Outcast" landed as ~60 designations that spared China's major banks, Treasury Secretary Bessent said a sanction on "a major financial institution" would come "before the end of this week," warned that Iran's backers would be "cut off from the US dollar system," and told Chinese banks they would be sanctioned if they "facilitate transactions and are part of the ecosystem that turns Iranian oil into money" — while still declining to name a Chinese bank; his own team called the rollout a "warning shot" (Bloomberg, CNBC, ABC News, Yahoo News). The list's contours sharpened: Iran's largest state bank Bank Melli was named for mandatory global shutdown of every branch, digital assets were added as a secondary-sanctions sector for the first time (with technology, gold, aviation, shipping), and dozens of mainland-Chinese and Hong Kong intermediaries plus teapot refineries were hit — but the big banks and yuan-clearing rails were again left standing (TechTimes, Forbes, Washington Post). China is the plan's hinge and is building its exit: analysts judged no "economic D-Day" without Beijing, which buys 80%+ of Iran's exported oil, whose teapots need no dollar access, and which is scaling its yuan-based CIPS as a dollar-clearing alternative atop the Day-179 blocking statute (Forbes, CNBC, China-Global South Project). A new mediator moved: Pakistan's army chief Field Marshal Asim Munir ended a Tehran mission (met Pezeshkian, Araghchi, Ghalibaf, Rezaei) carrying an MoU-revival proposal, though Rezaei told him the US "must change its behavior and take concrete steps" first (Arab News, Al Jazeera, Bloomberg). The market kept selling: Brent fell ~3% below $90 on the calibration read plus the mediation easing supply-shock fears (CNBC, Mansfield Energy). Lebanon frayed but quiet — no new Israeli operation and no executed Hezbollah reprisal through Aug 25; UNIFIL handed its Khardali-Litani position to the Lebanese Army's 7th Brigade Aug 20 (CNN). Direction holds mixed, tightening as the collision goes on a clock; 7-day risk critical; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds 5.
