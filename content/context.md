## Current war status

Day 175 was the day Washington's "economic isolation" plan drew organized return fire. A day after unveiling the plan and ruling out talks, the administration met open resistance from both targets: FM Araghchi dismissed the "Economic D-Day" as "economic terrorism" and "a diversion from America's own crisis — unprecedented debt and surging interest costs," reaffirming that talks and any Hormuz reopening require the US to end violations and pay compensation, while China's foreign ministry rejected the threat ("sanctions and pressure tactics are not the solution") and Beijing ordered firms to defy US sanctions on Iranian oil (Press TV, Al-Monitor, Fox News). Iran and Oman were reported to have finalized a Hormuz "shipping map" (northern Iranian inbound / southern Omani outbound lanes, 60 days, no fees) but Araghchi says it does not reopen the strait; the Gaza demilitarization mechanism drew no firm Israeli commitment.
