## Current war status

Day 197 brought the war's second chokepoint to the edge of Iranian control. Yemen's Houthis completed a rapid ground offensive to seize the Red Sea port of Mokha and the strategic islands of Perim/Mayun and the Greater and Lesser Hanish group, taking effective control of Yemen's Red Sea coastline and the approaches to Bab al-Mandeb — a severe setback for Saudi Arabia and its Yemeni allies that puts Iran and its proxies on course to command both maritime gates of the Arabian Peninsula at once, with Kpler-tracked Bab al-Mandeb traffic falling to ~12 vessels from ~30 earlier in the week (NBC News, NPR, Al Jazeera, The National). The same day, Saudi Arabia shut its East-West (Petroline) pipeline — the ~7 mb/d overland bypass around Hormuz to the Red Sea at Yanbu — after drones Riyadh said were launched from Iraq struck it in the Riyadh and Medina regions, causing injuries; Riyadh said it would not retaliate to give Baghdad time to act (CNN, UPI, CNBC). Both hedges against a closed Hormuz were compromised in a day. Against the escalation ran the week's first real diplomatic movement: Iran said it will meet GCC foreign ministers and Iraq in Salalah, Oman on Monday (Sep 14) to discuss Hormuz shipping, temporary routes and mine clearance — still with no US counterparty — and Brent, which had cleared $105, settled back 2.8% to $104.61 on the news even as it booked an 8.7% weekly gain (Bloomberg, The Business Standard, CNBC). The IAEA's UNSC referral (23-3, Russia/China/Niger opposed), the Lebanon reprisal cycle (IDF struck an underground Hezbollah complex at Ali al-Taher) and Iran's execution wave carried. Direction holds escalating; 7-day risk extreme; spillover critical; 30-day ceasefire probability holds at 2.
