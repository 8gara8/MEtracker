## Diplomatic state

Day 153 showed the diplomacy maturing and fraying at once. The Oman two-corridor Hormuz architecture is the most developed of the war and now has a widened mediation bench — US envoys Witkoff and Kushner alongside Qatar, Pakistan, Egypt and Oman — but the fee at its center is a three-way fault line (Gulf-backed voluntary fees; Iran demanding control and long-term guarantees while rejecting an equal split; Washington calling mandatory fees "illegal"), and Iran's Baghaei still denies a formal negotiation exists even as its FM channels operate. Per §3.5.3 this is process over agreement, and the process now sits atop a fractured pause: Iran fired on US bases in Jordan and the US and Saudi struck militias in Iraq the same week the parties were said to be "approaching" a Hormuz deal. The corridor still reaches neither Bab el-Mandeb — where the Houthis answer to Yemen's own ledger and eye a toll of their own — nor Israel, which stays outside the US-Iran framework.
